Program Structure
THE PPL SYSTEM

Push-Pull-Legs, 6 days a week. Each muscle group trains twice per week with adequate recovery. All working sets are 11+ reps for hypertrophy. Every session ends with a 10-min treadmill walk. Total time: under 55 minutes.

Why PPL works best: Heavy compound lifts trigger the highest anabolic hormone response of any training style. More muscle = higher resting metabolism = fat burning 24/7. The treadmill finisher keeps cardiovascular fitness improving without interfering with lifting recovery. 11+ rep targets build both strength and hypertrophy simultaneously.

Progressive Overload
PROGRESSION

How to keep gaining after the initial adaptation phase.

▶
The 2-for-2 Rule
Core overload principle
Overload▼
Hit the top rep range (e.g. 13+ reps) for 2 consecutive sessions with the same exercise → add weight next session. This is non-negotiable. No rep records = no weight increases.
▶
Weight Increments
How much to add
Load▼
Weight Increments
  • Barbell compounds (bench, incline): +2.5 kg per progression
  • Leg Press: +5 kg · Hack Squat: +5 kg
  • Calf raises: +2 kg · Leg curls/extensions: +2 kg
  • Cables and dumbbells: +1–2 kg
  • Overhead press: +2 kg per progression
▶
Deload Week — Every 4 Weeks
Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24
Recovery▼
Same PPL structure. Drop all weights 40%. Cut 1 set per exercise. No max effort. You always come back stronger after a deload.
The 2-for-2 Rule: 2 sessions in a row hitting top reps → add weight.
Deload: Every 4 weeks, drop 40% weight, reduce sets by 1.
Track everything: Log reps, sets, weight every session in the Tracker tab.
Regress when: Form breaks down or joint pain appears. Drop 20%.
Recovery System
RECOVER
OR FAIL

Lifting 6 days per week is high volume. Without systematic recovery, you'll overtrain, plateau, and get injured.

😴
Sleep 7–9 Hours
Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Same wake time daily, dark room, no screens 60 min before.
🥩
Protein — 152g/day
1.6g per kg bodyweight, spread across 4–5 meals. Within 90 min post-training for maximum muscle protein synthesis.
💧
Hydration 3.5–4L/day
Add 750ml per training hour. 2% dehydration = 10–15% strength loss.
🔄
Foam Rolling 10 min/day
Priority: IT band, quads, upper back, lats. 45–60 sec per area.
💊
Evidence-Based Supplements
Creatine monohydrate 5g/day. Vitamin D3 + K2. Magnesium glycinate. Omega-3.
🧘
Sunday — Full Rest
No gym. High cortisol from overtraining blocks fat loss and muscle gain.
